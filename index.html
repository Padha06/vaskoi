01 Technology Architecture // ProSE™ Platform 2026
Proprietary Biochemistry

Revolutionizing Proteomics with Spatial Expansion.

Legacy protein sequencing has long struggled with optical crowding, signal degradation, and post-translational modifications. Vaskoi physically expands peptide chains to read every residue with uncompromised fidelity.

Inspect Sequencing Stages →
01

Sample Preparation & Functionalization

Proteins and intact peptide fragments are functionalized using high-affinity molecular linkers attached selectively to terminal residues, preparing the biological library for controlled synthesis without degradation.

02

Ordered Molecular Expansion

Each amino acid is sequentially spaced along an engineered backbone matrix in its native linear sequence, eliminating molecular overcrowding and expanding physical resolution beyond optical diffraction limits.

03

Amino Acid & PTM Sequencing

The assembled Vaskoi ProSE macromolecule passes through high-bandwidth nanopores, measuring distinctive ionic current blockades that provide unbiased discrimination across all 20 natural amino acids and key PTMs.

Vaskoi Biopharma leverages spatial expansion of amino acids to achieve single-molecule discrimination of all 20 amino acids and post-translational modifications directly from raw biological fluids.
